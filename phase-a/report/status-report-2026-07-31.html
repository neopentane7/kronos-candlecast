Executive Summary

The project's objective is to fine-tune and conformally calibrate the open-source Kronos-small financial foundation model on Indian NSE equities, publish the checkpoint and calibration study, and ship a consumer forecasting product on top of it. The differentiating claim is not forecast accuracy but honest uncertainty: probability cones whose stated coverage matches their empirical coverage.

Two of seven Phase-A milestones are complete and have passed their acceptance criteria. The environment is provisioned and verified on the target hardware, and a validated 54-ticker, 113,068-bar NSE corpus is in place with schema enforcement shared between training and serving paths. Both milestones were delivered with measured evidence rather than assertion.

Competitive positioning is favourable and externally verifiable. Upstream Kronos has substantial traction (35.1k stars, 5.9k forks) but its documentation contains no treatment of uncertainty quantification, calibration, conformal prediction, or coverage. An open upstream issue requesting exactly this capability (reporting ~52% empirical coverage) remains unanswered by maintainers. The intended contribution addresses a genuine and unfilled gap.

The principal management concern is conversion risk, not technical risk. All work to date is foundational; no model has been trained and no forecast metric has been produced. Every differentiating claim remains a promise until Milestone A3 executes. Compounding this, the repository holds zero commits, so no artifact currently exists outside the developer's local disk and the project's own reproducibility requirement is unmet.

Architectural decision of record

The specification's dual-source data strategy was found to be unsound in practice and was revised with approval. yfinance back-adjusts prices for corporate actions while jugaad-data returns raw as-traded values; across aligned sessions the two differ by exactly 2.000000× on price and 0.500000× on volume around a 1:1 bonus. Because both series satisfy any schema one can write, the shared-schema mechanism did not deliver train/serve parity as originally assumed. Adopting the adjusted series everywhere secures parity by construction at negligible cost: on non-action days the sources agree on returns to five decimal places.

Progress to Date

Milestone A1 — Environment & smoke test · accepted

  • CUDA availability confirmed on target hardware; zero-shot forecast rendered end to end.
  • Peak inference VRAM measured at 133.7 MB against a 6 GB budget — roughly 45× headroom, materially de-risking the A3 evaluation grid and A4 training.
  • CI, pre-commit, ruff and pytest established at project start rather than retrofitted; the workflow's exact command sequence was rehearsed locally, including the torch-free install path.
  • Reproducibility scaffolding implemented: every run emits results/<timestamp>_<git-sha>/ with an auto-stamped disclaimer.

Milestone A2 — Data pipeline · accepted

  • 54 of 55 tickers ingested; 113,068 bars spanning 2018-01-01 to 2026-06-30, with a minimum of 2,090 rows per ticker against a 1,400-row acceptance bar.
  • Schema validation passes on 100% of partitions; DuckDB confirms zero zero-volume rows and an amount proxy error of exactly 0.0, making the turnover-proxy constraint mechanically enforced rather than conventional.
  • Date-based splits established without ticker leakage: train 79,966 · validation 13,283 · test 19,819 rows, all 54 tickers present in every split.
  • Nine deliberate-corruption tests confirm that malformed rows fail validation, satisfying the milestone's negative-path requirement.

Validated findings arising from the work

  • The close × volume turnover proxy tracks real NSE turnover with a median ratio of 1.0009, empirically vindicating a specification decision that had been taken on parity grounds alone.
  • Corporate-action detection was recalibrated against the corpus rather than assumed. The largest genuine overnight gap across 108,880 bars is 16.2%, versus −50% for an unadjusted bonus — a separation of roughly 34 points that a close-to-close rule cannot achieve, since real intraday moves reach 44.7%.
  • Three defects were identified in the upstream inference path by code inspection: absence of a KV cache, discarding of sampled paths before they can be consumed, and a causal-masking discrepancy between training and evaluation modes.

Current Blockers & Risk Assessment

Risk register — ordered by severity
RiskSeverityAnalysis & impactMitigation
No version control history Critical The repository contains zero commits. Reproducibility requires results traceable to a commit; run directories currently record nogit. Two milestones of work exist only on local disk, representing both an unmitigated loss exposure and an unmet project constraint. Initial commit before any further milestone work.
Acceptance criterion may be statistically unreachable Critical Milestone A5 requires empirical coverage within a ±2% band (78–82%). The test period supports roughly 12 non-overlapping horizon-length blocks, and cross-sectional correlation among large-cap Indian equities is high. Estimator noise plausibly exceeds the acceptance width, meaning a correct implementation could fail on sampling variance alone. Measure effective sample size during A3; report coverage with a block-bootstrap confidence interval and revise the criterion accordingly.
Validation loss measured under inconsistent attention High Upstream sets causal masking from the training flag, so one attention module is non-causal in evaluation mode. Milestone A4's acceptance depends on validation loss, which would be measured under a different attention pattern than training and is therefore optimistically biased and not comparable. Force causal masking for validation loss in the A4 overlay; expect the corrected figure to be less favourable.
Upstream training pipeline unusable as supplied High The upstream dataset class reads a single file, sorts globally by timestamp, slides windows across the whole frame, and splits by ratio. A multi-ticker corpus would be interleaved by date with windows spliced across ticker boundaries, and ratio splits cannot express date-based splits. Overlay dataset class scheduled for A4; export format and column ordering already accommodate it.
Evaluation throughput constrains study design Medium Benchmarking shows the generation loop reprocesses the full context at every step. The evaluation grid costs ~49 minutes at the specified stride and ~4.6 hours at the finer stride needed to stabilise coverage estimates — doubled, since A5 repeats the grid on the fine-tuned model. Implement KV caching with a numerical-equivalence test; measured ceiling is ~8×.
Planned comparator likely to fail its own criterion Medium Published benchmarking across 3,000+ series finds EnbPI — the specified A5 comparator — fails to attain nominal coverage, while MSCP achieves the best interval efficiency among valid methods and ACI ranks lowest. Substitute MSCP as the study comparator; retain ACI for the online serving path. Awaiting approval.
Calendar substitution and coverage gaps Medium The specified XNSE calendar does not exist in the library; XBOM was substituted and verified equivalent. Two residual limitations: the packaged holiday list terminates 2026-12-31, and Muhurat sessions are omitted, so calendar-generated timestamps cannot reproduce a session present in the data. Isolated to a single constant. Specification amendment pending; library upgrade required before year end.
Upstream contribution target mismatched Medium The A7 target issue concerns 1-minute intraday forecasting, whereas this project is scoped to daily bars with a 30-day horizon and explicitly defers intraday work. Methodology transfers; numerical results will not. Reframe the contribution as methodology plus daily-horizon evidence.
Single ticker excluded by validation Low One constituent carries an unadjusted demerger (−62.6% overnight gap) and is correctly rejected; a second was delisted upstream and substituted. Universe stands at 54, comfortably above the 45-ticker floor. No action. Exclusion is recorded with its reason in the manifest.
Assessment

No risk in this register threatens project viability. Two, however, threaten the credibility of the eventual result — the coverage-criterion feasibility issue and the validation-loss inconsistency — and both are cheaper to resolve now than to discover during A5. The critical version-control gap is remediable in minutes and should not survive the current session.
